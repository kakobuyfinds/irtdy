How to use the PikoBuy spreadsheet

  1. Open the product on CAFANS and copy the original seller URL. Paste it into PikoBuy and confirm the agent can load it.
  2. Check the exact color, size and price. For shoes, compare the seller’s insole measurements with shoes you already wear. For tops, compare chest width and body length laid flat.
  3. If warehouse quality-control (QC) photos are available, compare the size label, color and details with your order. Report any mismatch before approving dispatch.
  4. Get a shipping quote using the destination and packed parcel weight and dimensions. Check the agent’s fees and what the shipping quote covers.

PikoBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is a PikoBuy spreadsheet?

It’s a browsable list of product finds. Use this page to compare CAFANS items and follow their listings to find original seller links for use with a shopping agent.

Which warehouse QC photos are worth checking?

Look for clear photos of the size label and the chosen color. Check the soles and seams on sneakers, zipper alignment on jackets, and stitching around strap attachments on bags. The images above are catalog photos.

What if PikoBuy cannot open the seller link?

Open the original link in your browser and check whether the listing still loads and your variant is still available. If the seller page works, ask the agent whether it can process that listing before paying.
